What are Credits?

Credits are required in order for you to stay in National Honors Society for the next Semester

You need in total: Service: ; Project: ; Tutoring:

Make sure to check Google Classroom regularly for new service or project opportunities. Excess credits are not carried over to the next semester.

Credits can be transferred only for the fall semester, unless otherwise stated. 1 service credit is worth 2 project credits. 1 service credit is worth 1 tutoring credit. You can only make 3 service-tutoring credit conversions and 1 serivce-project credit conversion. Conversions of service-project credits are prioritized.

What are Tutoring Credits?

You can only tutor Bronx Science Students in order to obtain credit.

You can get tutoring credits by either tutoring one-on-one through a match by the NHS email "Tutoring Pairing Email", SGI tutoring with teacher's signature, or through Science for Bronx.

You submit your tutoring credits at the end of each semester via a google form.

Obtaining Credits

You can obtain service and prject credits by checking the NHS google classroom for assignments with sign-up sheets. Please mark all assignments as done, including ones irrelevant to you, as this allows us to know you have seen the information posted.

Service and project credits will be posted at either 7:30 AM or 4:30 PM. An email will be sent a day before to let you know when the events will be posted.

Sign up for service and project credits using the Sheet linked with the assignment. Sign up is first come first serve, so make sure to sign up quickly.

If there is any urgent service opportunities, we will post them as soon as possible.

Meeting Attendance

You must attend all NHS meetings.

You get one excused absence per year. You must fill out the excused absence form on google classroom 24 hours before a meeting.

All other excused absences will result in 1 probation. If you submit your excused absence form late (past 24 hours) you will recieve 1 probation.

Unexcused absences will result in 2 probations. A 2nd unexcused absence will result in you being removed from NHS

Probations

You must also not have more than 3 Probations. Once you have your 3rd Probation, you will be removed from NHS and will not be able to join back. You aquire probations by not attending meetings without being excused (2 Probations), not attending a service you signed up for (1 Probation), not responding to an email within 24 hours (1 Probation), and not submitting assignments on Google Classroom on time (1 Probation).

You have: Probations and unexcused absences from meetings

To clear 1 Probation, you must get either 2 SERVICE credits or 1 PROJECT credit. This credit will only be used for the probation and will not count towards its categories. Probations reset at the end of the Semester.
